Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you install.
Is Keyblind really local?

Yes. The vault runs entirely on your machine. There is no cloud dependency for core functionality and zero telemetry.

Which AI tools are supported?

Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, Cline, and Zed. Any tool that supports MCP.

Is the free plan limited?

You get full core functionality with up to 5 secrets. Most individual developers can use Free productively.
